    Hi, I'm

    Sushma Somashekar

    Designer. Brand storyteller. An Aquarius who has always asked why.

    As a kid, colour didn't just look nice. It made me feel things.

    • Red · powerful
    • Sky · like a bird
    • Moon · calm
    • Green · fresh

    Later I learned it had a name: colour theory. The same language brands use to speak to people. That's when I knew what I wanted to do.

    1. 2016

      Business first

      Love for colour alone wouldn't cut it. I did a BBA in Marketing at IMTS Institute to understand the business side of brands. Graduated with an A+.

    2. 2020

      Then, design

      An Advanced Diploma at JD Institute of Fashion Technology. 60% design, 40% fashion. I loved both.

    3. 2022

      A brand from scratch

      I co-founded C CYUB, a sustainable clothing brand. Sourcing, R&D, investor calls, socials. In 9 months, celebrities featured us and our clothes were on Bigg Boss Kannada.

    4. 2023

      Agency life

      My first official job, at Creating Conversion. Fanzart, WHIMS, Holiday Tribe, Tasteable, and Aira on weekends. I picked up Illustrator, Photoshop, Lightroom and Premiere Pro on the way.

    5. 2023

      Across the ocean

      A Canadian change management expert hired me to build her dream company, Nexa Avenue. A new culture, fully remote. Patience made it magic, and her network became my clients.

    6. 2025

      A blank page

      When that chapter closed, I didn't see an empty road. I saw a blank page. I filled it with books, journals and colouring books on Amazon KDP.

    7. 2025

      Five days later

      Credtech called. Two months of events: big-screen editorials, panel posters, floor plans, food menus and decor mood boards.

    8. Now

      Health IT

      Branding & Marketing Associate at Darena Health. A whole new field. The wins came a little late. When they came, they felt like stars.

    Still curious. Still asking why.
