Chapter one

The girl who always asked why

Since childhood, I have loved art, colour and texture. Not randomly. I loved them in a certain way, when they were arranged through the principles of design. They made me feel something.

  • Red makes me feel powerful
  • Sky makes me feel like a bird
  • The moon makes me feel calm
  • Green makes me feel fresh

Soon I realised this was colour theory, the same language brands use to speak to people. I wanted to help brands. Love for colour alone wouldn't cut it, so I studied marketing to understand the business side. Then I dived into design: a course that was 60% design and 40% fashion. I loved both.

Since then, I have built a clothing brand from scratch, worked with brands across four countries and learned a whole new field in Health IT. Every chapter started the same way. I asked why.

  • 5+years in design and marketing
  • 10+brands across fashion, food, weddings and health tech
  • 4countries: India, Canada, USA, Australia

  2. Mar 2025 to Apr 2025 · Bangalore

    Credtech

    Sales & Marketing Manager

    An edtech role that added an event management badge to my career. I designed big-screen editorials, cue cards, IDs, panel posters, invites, newsletters, the floor plan, the food menu and the decor mood board.

  3. 2025 · Self-published

    Amazon KDP

    Author & Designer

    When one chapter in Canada closed, I saw a blank page, not an empty road. I couldn't let a day pass without creating, so I turned the pause into books, journals and colouring books on Amazon KDP. Five days of reaching out later, the next door opened.

  4. Nov 2023 to Mar 2025 · Toronto, Canada · Remote

    Nexa Avenue

    Digital Marketing Manager

    A Canadian business consultant and change management expert hired me full time to build her dream company. It was my first time working overseas, with someone from a very different culture. Patience and the right mindset made it easy. Through her, I went on to work with more Canadian business owners on social content, presentations, mood boards, websites, documents and books.

  5. Feb 2023 to Oct 2023 · Bangalore
