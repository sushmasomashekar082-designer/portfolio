# Bonus coin notes (drafts)

Each project hides a coin. Opening it shows three short lines. I wrote these only from what you've told me, so edit anything that doesn't sound like you.

| Project | Why I chose it | What I learnt | Fun fact |
|---|---|---|---|
| Darena Health | Health IT is complex. I wanted to make it feel human. | When the website is slow, learn to fix it yourself. So I hand-coded the new pages. | MyMipsScore went from 57 to 94 on PageSpeed. My proudest number. |
| Nexa Avenue | My first brand across an ocean, for a consultancy in Toronto. | Time zones are a design problem too. Analytics told me what to post next. | 184 reels and 92 posts. My camera roll has not recovered. |
| C CYUB | A friend and I wanted clothes that felt good and did good. So we started our own label. | Founding teaches you everything at once: sourcing, R&D, investor calls, photoshoots and the website. | Nine months in, our clothes were on Bigg Boss Kannada. |
| Fanzart | Making a luxury fan feel emotional is a fun challenge. | A festival story can sell a ceiling fan. #SilentPromises proved it. | Reels for a fan brand passed 70K views. The fans really did blow up. |
| WHIMS | Slow fashion and capsule wardrobes. A cause I already believed in. | For a young, conscious audience, say less and mean more. | I wrote the launch roadmap before the brand had even launched. |
| Tasteables | An Australian home chef app. Food, stories and a new market. | Every dish should feel like an invitation, not a menu. | Writing about food on an empty stomach is a real job hazard. |
| Holiday Tribe | Travel content is pure feeling. I wanted to capture that. | Show the moment, not the itinerary. | Bali, in 24 seconds. Proof you can travel through an editing timeline. |
| Aira Weddings | Weddings are colour, style and emotion in one day. I did this on weekends, for joy. | A mood board is a promise of how a day will feel. | 2K+ new organic followers in 3 months. |
| Amazon KDP | After a layoff, I wanted to make something fully mine. | When one door closes, design a new cover. | I am a published author of journals and colouring books. |
| Credtech | One big event, every touchpoint, two months. | Print has no undo button. Check the file five times. | Pune Chapter 2025 ran on my designs, from invites to the big screen. |
| Nektar Homes | A home stay brand that needed a face from scratch. | A brand kit is a promise of consistency. | Logo, flyer and reels: a whole brand that hums like a bee. |
